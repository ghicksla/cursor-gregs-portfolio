The Signature Framework

The Vibecoding Development Life Cycle (VDLC)

Vibe coding isn't just "talking to a chatbot and praying." Greg equips your teams with the disciplined, 4-stage engine that turns gut instincts into board-grade software.

01

Research

Understand Customer Pain

Define the real pain point through Follow-Me-Homes, telemetry, and stakeholder interviews. Ground the AI with deep contextual reality instead of assumptions.

Output: Problem Statement & Context
02

Design

AI-Partnered PRD SOT

Partner with advanced reasoning models (Gemini 2.5 Pro / GPT-4o) to co-author clear requirements, architecture flows, UI layouts, and edge cases.

Output: Single Source of Truth PRD
03

Plan

Decompose Into Chunks

Break down the vision into manageable, testable phases. Build in micro-increments: prevent the AI from hallucinating or generating bloated unmaintainable code.

Output: Chunked Execution Roadmap
04

Implement

Interactive Clickable App

Execute conversational vibe coding in live Canvas mode. Prompt, preview, pressure-test, and refine in real-time. Prototypes replace 30-page requirement docs.

Output: Live Browser Application

"Prototypes > Requirements specs. If you can describe what you need, you can build it."
